PACKING SLIP CHEF SYSTEM — WINDOWS

1. Right-click this ZIP and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click START_WINDOWS.bat.
4. Keep the black window open.
5. The app should open at:
   http://localhost:8501

IF THE BROWSER DOES NOT OPEN
Open Chrome or Edge yourself and type:
   http://localhost:8501

IF THAT PAGE SAYS IT CANNOT CONNECT
Look at the black window for an error and send a screenshot of it in the ChatGPT conversation.

IMPORTANT
Do not double-click app.py. Use START_WINDOWS.bat.
